Wiggle Out

Unblock the trapped worms. Tap the arrow whose path is clear.

Every puzzle is a crowd of arrow-worms shaped into a letter, a number or a symbol. An arrow can only leave if nothing blocks the way its head is facing. Tap the right one and it wriggles free. Tap a blocked one and the arrow that was in its way lights up, so you learn instead of guessing.

No score to chase, no combo screaming at you — just the quiet satisfaction of spotting the way out. No twitch reflexes and no coins to spend. It works offline.

82 levels in 5 chapters:

  • Letters — grids shaped like A to Z
  • Numbers — 0 to 9
  • Animals — dog, fox, panda and 17 more
  • Shapes — geometric and emoji silhouettes
  • Math — operators, comparisons and equations

Available in 9 languages, with the character spoken aloud in each.
