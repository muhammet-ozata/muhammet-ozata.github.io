Worm Out!

A fun and colorful arrow puzzle game. Tap the right arrows in the right order to clear the board.

Worm Out! is a free puzzle game with 62 hand-crafted levels across 4 categories — letters, numbers, shapes and math. Available in 9 languages. Combine quick reflexes with logic to chase a perfect 3-star score.
